Ongoing projects

Studies and programs we are running now. If you or someone you care for is recovering from ACL reconstruction or another knee procedure, you may be able to take part.

Enrolling

SPRINT

Intense, small-group exercise sessions that bridge the gap between physical therapy and a return to sport and activity.

  • Who
    • Had ACL reconstruction or another knee procedure
    • Beginning the move from physical therapy to sport or intense physical activity
  • Format
    • Pre-program assessment
    • 8 small-group training sessions, 75 minutes each
    • Post-program assessment
  • Led by
    • Two physical therapists
  • Where
    • Charlottesville, VA
  • Support
    • Jefferson Trust

Opening December 2026

Mind in Motion

A 6-week, phone-based program that pairs short mindfulness sessions with personalized daily activity goals from a wearable ring.

  • Who
    • Ages 15 to 30
    • 1 to 5 years after a first ACL reconstruction on one knee
    • Still worried about reinjury and less active than before surgery
    • Medically cleared for activity
    • Has a compatible smartphone
  • Format
    • 2 short in-person visits
    • 6 weeks at home
    • 5 ten-minute Headspace sessions a week
    • Oura Ring for daily activity goals
    • Short follow-up 4 weeks later
  • Where
    • Student Health and Wellness Center, Charlottesville, VA
  • Support
    • UVA TYDE
  • Registered
    • ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07912502
