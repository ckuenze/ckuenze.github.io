SPRINT

Intense, small-group exercise sessions that bridge the gap between physical therapy and a return to sport and activity.

  • Who
    • Had ACL reconstruction or another knee procedure
    • Beginning the move from physical therapy to sport or intense physical activity
  • Format
    • Pre-program assessment
    • 8 small-group training sessions, 75 minutes each
    • Post-program assessment
  • Led by
    • Two physical therapists
  • Where
    • Charlottesville, VA
  • Support
    • Jefferson Trust

Mind in Motion

The purpose of the study is to test a phone-based program that combines short mindfulness exercises with personalized daily activity goals to learn whether the program changes confidence and physical activity after ACL surgery.

  • Who
    • Ages 15 to 30
    • History of ACL surgery 1 to 5 years ago
  • Lab visits
    • 3 visits to the laboratory
    • Complete surveys about your knee, physical activity, and how you feel about movement
    • Have your height and weight measured
    • Be fitted with a smart ring (Oura Ring) to wear daily and a thigh-mounted fitness tracker (activPAL) to wear for 7 days
    • The study team will also ask to review your medical records for research purposes
  • At home
    • A 6-week home-based program to increase your activity
    • Use the Mind in Motion smartphone app, including guided mindfulness sessions and personalized daily activity goals
    • Complete two brief daily check-in surveys on your phone each morning and evening for 6 weeks
    • Complete 5 short mindfulness sessions per week through the app for 6 weeks
    • Complete online surveys again, 4 weeks after the program ends
  • Compensation
    • $50 Amazon gift card and $200 in checks for study completion
  • Where
    • Department of Kinesiology, University of Virginia, Charlottesville, VA
  • Contact
    • Chris Kuenze · cmk7sq@virginia.edu · (434) 924-1451
    • Study flyer (PDF) · IRB-HSR #303329
  • Support
    • UVA TYDE
  • Registered
    • ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07912502
