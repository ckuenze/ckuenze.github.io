Teaching

Interactive tools for my classes

I build small, browser-based tools that let students see biomechanics and research methods in action. They are free to use, run in any modern browser with nothing to install, and are open source on GitHub.

Example data in these tools are simulated for teaching. Videos loaded into the video tools stay on your own device.
