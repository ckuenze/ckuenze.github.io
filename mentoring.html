Prospective students

Interested in joining the lab?

I recruit doctoral students through the Sports Medicine concentration of UVA's Kinesiology PhD program. Strong applicants are curious about recovery after ACL injury and comfortable working with patients, data, and collaborators across many sites. Please read a few of our recent papers, then send a short note about your interests and experience.
